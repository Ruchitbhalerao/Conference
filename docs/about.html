About the Conference

ICIPTT 2027 advances dialogue on how intellectual property and technology transfer turn research into innovation that serves society and the economy.

The conference provides an international platform for scholars and practitioners to share research, policy insights and practical experience in managing, protecting and commercializing knowledge.

Its objectives are to strengthen collaboration between academia and industry, promote best practice in technology transfer, and inform the next generation of IP policy.

  • Intellectual Property
  • Technology Transfer
  • Innovation
  • Research Commercialization
  • Knowledge Exchange
  • Industry–Academia Collaboration

Conference Theme

From Knowledge to Impact: IP, Innovation & the Future of Technology Transfer

The theme explores how intellectual property frameworks and technology-transfer practice can turn research into societal and economic value — across institutions, sectors and borders.
